A self-reflection exercise

Where would you place your AI estate?

Ten questions, about three minutes. They walk through estate visibility, governance discipline, and production value, and place your answers on the quadrant from Chapter 5 of Govern or Fail.

What this is not. It is not a score, an audit, a benchmark, or a validated instrument. Every answer is self-reported and nothing is checked against your estate. The quadrant itself is, in the book's words, a diagnostic lens for portfolio decisions, not a maturity model or scoring method — and the book applies it system by system, not across an estate on average.

What it is for. Getting a conversation started with the people who would have to defend a challenged decision, and finding out whether they would place the organisation where you would. The result is a prompt, not a finding.

Free, and no email needed for your result. Your answers never leave your browser; only your final position is submitted, and only if you ask for the matching edition by email.

Looking for the instruments the book actually points to? The customer challenge test, the technical control test, the four prerequisites and the Chapter 11 scenario model are on the resources page, ungated.
