Regulatory timeline

EU AI Act & Digital Omnibus

The book's source note [15] and Appendix C carry the position as enacted. Nothing below supersedes them; it restates the current status in one place.

  • 24 July 2026

    The Digital Omnibus on AI — proposed as COM(2025) 836 final — was enacted as Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 and published in the Official Journal. It entered into force on 27 July 2026, the third day following publication. It is no longer a proposal or a provisional agreement.

  • 2 August 2026

    The AI Act's general application date is unmoved. Article 50 generally applies from this date. Providers of AI systems generating synthetic audio, image, video or text content placed on the market before it have until 2 December 2026 to comply with the machine-readable marking requirements of Article 50(2); that transitional provision does not postpone the other Article 50 obligations.

  • 2 December 2027

    Application of the relevant high-risk requirements for Article 6(2) / Annex III systems, as fixed by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744. The original date was 2 August 2026.

  • 2 August 2028

    Application of the relevant high-risk requirements for Article 6(1) / Annex I product-embedded systems, as fixed by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744. The original date was 2 August 2027.

Readers should verify the applicable provisions, exceptions and transitional arrangements against the consolidated AI Act and the Official Journal text at the time of reading, as implementation guidance continues to develop. Nothing on this page is legal advice.

Instrument versions

The Debt Quadrant self-reflection exercise

  • v2.0 — current, 22 September 2026

    Position labels aligned to the book's Figure 5.1 — Governed value, Value with exposure, Controlled but low-value, Noise or residue. Scoring corrected: estate visibility now gates the higher-governance-discipline row, so a result can no longer report demonstrated discipline over an estate the respondent cannot see. A visibility-gap note now appears for any respondent scoring low on visibility, regardless of where they placed themselves. Framing changed throughout from "assessment" to a self-reflection exercise, as the book treats the quadrant as a diagnostic lens for portfolio decisions, not a maturity model or scoring method.

  • v1.1 — 6 July 2026

    Initial published version. Used the labels Governed, Reckless, Stagnant and Dormant, which are not the book's. Its visibility questions were collected but did not affect the computed position — a respondent reporting no AI inventory could still be placed in the highest position. Superseded by v2.0; the PDF editions carrying the old labels are being reissued.
