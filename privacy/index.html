Legal

Privacy policy

Plain language, because that is the whole point of this book. Last updated 6 July 2026.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Fabio Aulico, trading as Guardrail Press, based in Switzerland. Questions about this policy or your data go to hello@governorfail.com.

What we collect

Your email address, and only when you type it into a signup form yourself. What else is stored depends on where you sign up:

  • If you sign up from a general form (home page or an essay), we store only your email address.
  • If you sign up from the result screen of the self-reflection exercise, we store your email address and one short code for your final position (for example reckless, the internal code for "Value with exposure"), so we can send the matching edition. Your individual answers are not stored.

We do not ask for your name, we do not buy lists, and we collect nothing else through the form.

The assessment stays in your browser

The Quadrant assessment runs entirely on your device. Your individual answers are never transmitted to us or anyone else. Only if you choose to submit the email form on the result screen do your email address and your final result position leave your browser.

Why we collect it

To send you your edition of the Debt Quadrant instrument, to send occasional essays, and to tell you when something in the book has been overtaken and the updates page has changed. That is the entire purpose. The legal basis is your consent, given when you confirm your subscription.

Double opt-in

After you sign up, we send one email asking you to confirm. If you do not click to confirm, we do nothing further and your address is not added to the list.

To be precise about the order of events: your PDF download starts immediately, before you confirm. Confirming is what adds you to the mailing list, not what releases the file. If you never confirm, you keep the download and hear nothing more from us.

Who processes your email

Our email is handled by Kit (Kit.com, Inc.), based in the United States. Storing your address with Kit means it is transferred to and processed in the US; Kit provides the safeguards required for such transfers. We do not share your address with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe. Every email includes a one-click unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes you from the list.

Your rights

Under the EU GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP) you can request access to your data, correct it, or have it deleted, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Use the unsubscribe link in any email, or write to hello@governorfail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits. It is cookieless, sets nothing on your device, and collects no personal data — which is why this site needs no cookie banner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version and its date appear on this page.
