The argument

What the book argues

Enterprise AI usually fails after the demo, not at it. The model works in the pilot; the organisation cannot govern what the pilot becomes once it reaches production, touches real data, and starts making decisions someone is accountable for.

The recurring cause is governance debt: the future cost of deploying AI capability before ownership, boundaries, monitoring, evidence, and accountability have been designed into the system. Unclear ownership, unmanaged data boundaries, missing runtime evidence, shadow AI, and policy that reads well but cannot be proven in operation.

The claim is deliberately narrow. Not every failed AI project is a governance failure — weak use cases, insufficient model capability, poor product design, bad economics, unusable data, and absent adoption all remain distinct causes, and governance rescues none of them. Govern or Fail concerns technically useful AI that cannot survive production because the enterprise never established the ownership, boundaries, evidence, controls, and recovery needed to operate it.

“Governance is not the document beside enterprise AI. It is the operating condition that allows enterprise AI to remain useful, defensible, recoverable, and scalable.”

Written for the CIOs, CTOs, CISOs, and the risk, audit, and enterprise-architecture leaders who carry the outcomes.

Author

About the author

Fabio Aulico works at the intersection of enterprise technology, integration, AI governance, and business transformation. His career spans more than eighteen years in enterprise technology, including engineering, integration, consulting, delivery, professional services, and commercial roles supporting executive technology decisions.

Govern or Fail is written from a practitioner vantage point: not as a manifesto against AI, and not as a technology provider playbook, but as a diagnostic field report for executives who need AI to become an operating capability rather than a sequence of impressive pilots.

He holds a Master of Advanced Studies in Management, Technology and Economics (MAS MTEC) from ETH Zürich and has completed AI strategy executive education at London Business School. He completed professional training in AI governance through the Swiss Cyber Institute.

Govern or Fail is published by Guardrail Press, an independent imprint focused on operationally honest writing about enterprise AI.
